# CCS Glossary

Compact definitions, grouped by chapter. Acronyms expanded once.

## Energy and Climate (Ch 1-3)
- **Airborne fraction**: share of emitted CO2 that stays in the atmosphere in a given year. Currently ~45% of 9 Gt C/yr.
- **Anthropogenic**: caused by human activity, as opposed to natural fluxes.
- **AOSIS**: Alliance of Small Island States, vocal in UN climate negotiations.
- **Biological pump**: biologically driven export of particulate carbon from surface to deep ocean; sequesters CO2 for centuries.
- **Blackbody**: idealized perfect emitter/absorber; Earth equivalent T ~255 K vs surface 288 K -> 33 K greenhouse effect.
- **Carbon reservoirs**: atmosphere 720 Gt C, surface ocean 670, deep ocean 36,730, terrestrial 2,000, fossil fuels 4,130, sedimentary carbonates >60M.
- **Committed emissions**: future CO2 from existing power plants, vehicles, infrastructure. Davis et al. 2010: 496 Gt CO2 -> 1.1-1.4 C warming.
- **Common but differentiated responsibilities (CBDR)**: UNFCCC principle that developed countries bear greater mitigation burden.
- **Glacial-interglacial CO2 cycle**: 180 ppm (glacial) to 280 ppm (interglacial), driven by ocean circulation and biological pump.
- **Keeling curve**: continuous Mauna Loa CO2 record since 1958; crossed 400 ppm in 2013.
- **PETM (Paleocene-Eocene Thermal Maximum)**: ~55 Ma, 1000-2000 Gt C release, 5 C warming, 100 kyr recovery. Closest analogue to anthropogenic emissions.
- **Preformed nutrients**: deep-ocean nutrients not used by biology at the surface; signal of inefficient biological pump (Sigman).
- **Radiative forcing**: change in net radiative flux at tropopause; CO2 doubling = +3.7 W/m^2.
- **SRES scenarios**: IPCC emission families A1FI/A1B/A1T/A2/B1/B2; A1FI fossil-intensive, B1 sustainability-oriented.
- **Stabilization wedge (Pacala-Socolow)**: 1 Gt C/yr emissions reduction over 50 yr. Originally 7 wedges, Davis 2013 updated to 31.
- **UNFCCC**: United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change (1992).

## Capture (Ch 4-7)
- **Air separation unit (ASU)**: cryogenic distillation of O2 from air; 0.4 GJ/t O2 energy cost. Required for oxy-fuel.
- **Barrer**: permeability unit, 1 Barrer = 3.348x10^-19 kmol*m/(m^2*s*Pa).
- **Bottoms 1930**: R.R. Bottoms patent for MEA-based CO2 absorption; foundational flowsheet.
- **Carbamate pathway**: primary/secondary amine + CO2 -> RNHCOO- + RNH3+, capacity 0.5 mol CO2/mol amine.
- **Carnot efficiency**: max thermal efficiency = 1 - T_c/T_h. PC plant ~35%, NGCC ~60%.
- **Chemical looping combustion (CLC)**: metal oxide (NiO, Fe2O3) shuttles O2 from air to fuel, producing pure CO2 + H2O.
- **Econamine FG+**: Fluor commercial MEA-based post-combustion package.
- **Henry's law**: y = kappa*x, kappa = K_CO2/P. Pure water at 25 C, 1 atm: kappa ~ 1600.
- **Hindered amine (KS-1)**: MHI/KEPCO commercial solvent; bulky R-groups destabilize carbamate -> ~1.0 mol/mol capacity.
- **IGCC (Integrated Gasification Combined Cycle)**: coal -> syngas -> H2 + CO2 at 40-60% CO2 partial pressure; pre-combustion capture.
- **Langmuir isotherm**: theta = bp/(1+bp). Henry regime at low p: sigma = sigma_max*b*p.
- **McCabe-Thiele**: graphical staging on y-x diagram for absorber design.
- **MEA (monoethanolamine)**: primary amine, 30 wt% aqueous; benchmark solvent. Heat of absorption ~85 kJ/mol CO2.
- **Membrane stage cut theta**: fraction of feed that permeates; small theta -> high purity low recovery.
- **Minimum work of separation**: thermodynamic floor for capture. For 90% capture from 12% CO2: ~158 kJ/kg CO2.
- **Parasitic energy**: power diverted to capture + compression. E_par = 0.75*eta_final*Q + W_comp.
- **Polaris**: MTR commercial post-combustion membrane; P'_CO2 ~1000 GPU, alpha ~30.
- **Piperazine**: concentrated aqueous promoter; faster kinetics than MEA.
- **Rectisol**: methanol physical solvent; operates at -40 to -62 C.
- **Selexol (DEPG)**: dimethyl ethers of polyethylene glycol; physical solvent for high-pressure gas treating.
- **Solution-diffusion**: dominant membrane transport mechanism; j = D*H/L*(pR - pP).
- **TSA/PSA/VSA**: Temperature/Pressure/Vacuum Swing Adsorption. VSA preferred for post-combustion.
- **Working capacity**: sigma(adsorb) - sigma(regenerate). Design number, NOT saturation capacity.

## Storage (Ch 8-10)
- **Capillary entry pressure**: pressure required for CO2 to invade caprock pore throats. Sets max column height.
- **Caprock**: low-permeability seal (shale, evaporite); k <10^-18 m^2.
- **Closure**: dome/anticline structure that confines buoyant CO2.
- **CO2-EOR**: Enhanced Oil Recovery with CO2 flooding; Permian Basin mature industry.
- **Critical point (CO2)**: 31 C, 7.4 MPa. Reached at ~800 m depth -> supercritical.
- **Darcy's law (two-phase)**: u_i = -(k*k_r,i/mu_i)*(grad P_i - rho_i*g).
- **ECBM**: Enhanced Coalbed Methane; CO2 displaces adsorbed CH4.
- **Frio I**: Texas pilot, 1,600 t CO2, 1.5 km depth (2004).
- **Hysteresis**: drainage vs imbibition give different k_r curves; essential for residual trapping.
- **In Salah**: Algeria, 1.2 Mt/yr into 1800 m sandstone; suspended 2011 over leakage concerns.
- **Joule-Thomson cooling**: positive JT coefficient for CO2 at storage P,T; expansion cools -> can form liquid CO2.
- **Mount Simon**: US Midwest saline formation; major storage target.
- **Otway**: CO2CRC pilot (Australia), 65,000 t CO2; blind prediction exercise.
- **Relative permeability**: k_r,i(S_i); Corey or Brooks-Corey parametrization.
- **Residual trapping**: CO2 ganglia immobilized by capillary snap-off at trailing edge of plume; ~20% over decades.
- **Sleipner**: Norway, 1 Mt/yr into Utsira Fm since 1996; longest dedicated saline storage.
- **Snohvit**: Norway sub-seabed, 0.7 Mt/yr since 2008.
- **Spill point**: lowest elevation where CO2 could escape laterally from closure.
- **Supercritical CO2**: T > 31 C, P > 7.4 MPa; density 600-750 kg/m^3 at reservoir conditions.
- **TOUGH2 / ECO2N**: Lawrence Berkeley Lab simulator; standard for CO2-brine reservoirs.
- **Utsira Formation**: storage reservoir at Sleipner, 800-1000 m depth.
- **Wettability**: sandstone water-wet (CO2 non-wetting); affects residual trapping.
- **Weyburn-Midale**: Canadian EOR with anthropogenic CO2 (coal gasification) since 2000.

## Removal and Geoengineering (Ch 11)
- **Afforestation**: planting forests on land without prior trees; ~3 t C/ha/yr temperate rate.
- **Biochar**: pyrolyzed biomass carbon; 35-50% char yield at 400 C slow pyrolysis.
- **Bio-oil**: liquid product of fast pyrolysis; 60-70% yield at 700 C.
- **Cement**: calcination CaCO3 -> CaO + CO2 at 900 C; 60% of cement emissions.
- **DAC (Direct Air Capture)**: capture from 0.04% CO2; thermodynamic minimum ~500 kJ/mol.
- **Enhanced weathering**: accelerating natural silicate weathering with mined olivine ((Mg,Fe)2SiO4).
- **HNLC**: High Nutrient Low Chlorophyll ocean regions; Fe-limited.
- **Ocean fertilization**: Fe addition to HNLC regions; Redfield C:N:P:Fe = 106:16:1:0.001.
- **Redfield ratio**: stoichiometry of marine plankton; C:N:P = 106:16:1.
- **SRM (Solar Radiation Management)**: stratospheric sulfate aerosols; 1.8% sunlight reduction offsets 4 W/m^2.
- **Termination shock**: rapid warming if SRM is interrupted while CO2 remains high.
